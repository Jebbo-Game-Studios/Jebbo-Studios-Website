THE GAME

How long can you survive?

Gravity Labs is a fast-paced endless runner built around movement, timing and survival.

Jump, dodge and react as the world constantly challenges you. The further you travel, the harder things become.

Chase a higher score, learn the obstacles and push your run further than before.
