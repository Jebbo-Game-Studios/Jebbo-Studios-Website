THE STUDIO

Small studio. Big ideas.

Jebbo Game Studios is an independent game development studio focused on creating original games for players across mobile and PC.

We're interested in games with strong identities — whether that's a quick, addictive mobile experience or a mysterious world waiting to be explored.

Our current projects use both Unity and Unreal Engine, allowing us to choose the right technology for each game.
